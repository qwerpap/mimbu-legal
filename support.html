Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to netkaktakto@gmail.com — we answer within two working days. In the app, Settings › Write to us adds your app version automatically.

Restore purchases

Open Mimbu › Settings › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with.

Cancel a subscription

iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Mimbu › Cancel Subscription.

Where is my data?

Only on your iPhone. There is no account. Deleting the app deletes your entries.
