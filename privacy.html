Privacy Policy

Mimbu ("the app") is a contraction timer and a baby feeding, sleep and diaper log. This policy explains what happens to your information.

What stays on your device

Everything you log — contraction times, feeds, bottle amounts, sleep, diapers, your baby's name, due or birth date and quiz answers — is stored only on your iPhone. We have no account system and no server that receives this data. We cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases and analytics

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple. We use Adapty (adapty.io) to validate purchases, show subscription offers and count anonymous purchase and paywall events. Adapty receives an anonymous app-specific profile ID, your device's App Store receipt information, country, app version and device model. It does not receive anything you log in the app. Advertising identifiers (IDFA) and IP address collection are disabled. See Adapty's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders (the trial ending reminder and optional feed reminders) are scheduled locally on your device.

No ads, no tracking

Mimbu shows no advertising and does not track you across apps or websites. We do not sell or share personal information.

Health information

Mimbu is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice. It does not use Apple Health (HealthKit).

Children

Mimbu is meant for parents and caregivers, not for use by children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Feedback emails

If you write to us, we receive your email address and message, plus the app version and anonymous profile ID the app adds, and use them only to answer you.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
