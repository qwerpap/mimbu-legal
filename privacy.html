Privacy

What Mimbu keeps, what leaves your phone, and who sees what.

Where do my contraction times and feeds go?

Nowhere. Contractions, feeds, bottle amounts, naps, diapers, your baby's name, due or birth date and the answers you give while setting up are saved in the app's own database on your iPhone. We run no sign-in system and no server that receives them, so we cannot read them. Delete Mimbu and they are gone.

What happens when I share a summary?

Pro can turn a stretch of your log into plain text. That text only leaves the phone if you hand it to the iOS share sheet yourself, and then it goes wherever you send it (a message to your midwife, say). Mimbu does not send it anywhere on its own.

Who sees my purchases?

Apple processes every payment; Mimbu never sees a card number. To check which plan is active, show the right offer and count paywall views, we use the service Adapty. It receives a random profile id for this install, the App Store receipt data, your country, app version and device model. IP address collection is switched off, and nothing you log is sent along.

Which notifications does it send?

Only if you allow them: a heads-up before a free trial ends, and the feed reminder in Pro. Both are scheduled on the phone itself; there is no push server.

Does the app use the camera, microphone, location or Apple Health?

No. Mimbu asks for none of them and does not read HealthKit.

Is there advertising or tracking?

No ads, no ad identifiers, no tracking across other apps or sites, and no selling of personal data.

What if I write to you?

Your email address and message reach us, plus the app version and the random profile id the Settings feedback button adds. We use them to reply and for nothing else.

Is the app for children?

No, it is for parents and caregivers, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

What if this page changes?

The date at the bottom will change with it.
